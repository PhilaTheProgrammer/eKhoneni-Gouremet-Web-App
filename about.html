ABOUT US

Who Are We?

You might be asking yourself who are we, who is Ekhoneni Gourmet!? Well,the story is long and true filled with many challenges and small few wins that propelled us to where we are today. Ekhoneni Gourmet is the local fast food resturant but it wasn't always what you see it today. It started as a snack shop making it's food within a large cargo container. The owner couldn't afford their own building but they had strong desire to give his community the kind of food they would love to eat. Soon the locals would catch on to the restuarants tasty foods and give it the power to elevate itself and provide a better service and here we are.

Mission

What is our Mission?

Ekhoneni Gourmet is a mission to provide fast, delicious high quality food to the locals. The kind of foods they enjoy to eat with familiar menus and unique recipes. With this in mind, we will soon provide for the general public in South Africa once. Once we open these branches the resturant will be all too familiar to you.
